# SPEC: MIO-008 — ledger: append-only entries, idempotent movements, a balance that cannot go negative

Card: board/1-in-spec/MIO-008-coin-ledger.md · Revision 1 · Author: agent (Claude), 2026-11-20

## Context

Step 5 of Part B and the hard half of Part C. Everything that moves coins calls this module, so
the invariants belong here — and in the schema, where an application bug cannot reach them.

Governed by [ADR-0005](../decisions/ADR-0005-ledger-invariants-in-the-database.md).

## Decisions taken here

- **Coins are integers.** No fractional coin exists, so there is no rounding question.
- **Entries are insert-only; a correction is a compensating entry.** An audit trail you can
  rewrite is not one.
- **Every movement carries an idempotency key derived from its cause** (`spin:<id>`,
  `redemption:<id>`, `redemption:<id>:refund`). A caller that cannot name one is telling you the
  operation is not safely retryable.
- **The friendly error and the invariant are different things.** The application reads the
  balance to answer `422` cleanly; the `CHECK (coins >= 0)` is what makes overdrawing impossible.

## Behaviour spec

- **S1 — a credit raises the balance and leaves an entry.** Crediting 100 coins to a player with
  no history gives a balance of 100 and one `credit` entry carrying its reason and key.
- **S2 — a debit lowers it.** Debiting 40 from 100 leaves 60 and a second entry.
- **S3 — a debit beyond the balance is refused and changes nothing.** Debiting 150 from 100
  raises an `insufficient-balance` error carrying the balance and the amount, leaves the balance
  at 100, and writes no entry.
- **S4 — the database refuses a negative balance even when the application does not ask it to.**
  A direct `UPDATE ledger.balance SET coins = -1` is rejected by the constraint.
- **S5 — a replayed movement moves nothing twice.** Crediting with an idempotency key already
  used returns the first entry, leaves the balance unchanged, and adds no second entry. The same
  holds for a debit.
- **S6 — concurrent debits cannot overdraw.** Two simultaneous debits of 60 against a balance of
  100: exactly one succeeds, the other is refused, and the balance ends at 40.
- **S7 — entries are append-only.** `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on `ledger.entry` are refused by the
  database.
- **S8 — an unknown player has a balance of zero, and asking does not create a row.**
- **S9 — the history reads newest first and is scoped to the player.**
- **S10 — tenant isolation.** Tenant B sees balance zero for tenant A's player, cannot debit it,
  and the same idempotency key in two tenants is two independent movements.

## Open questions

- **Q1 — may a credit be negative to express a correction?** → **Answered** (agent default,
  2026-11-20): no. `coins > 0` is a constraint on every entry, and a correction is a `debit` with
  a reason. A signed amount makes every aggregate over the table a question of sign conventions.
- **Q2 — what reconciles `balance` against the sum of entries?** → **Answered** (agent default,
  2026-11-20): nothing yet, and that is TD-0001. They can only diverge through a bug, but nothing
  would tell us — so a nightly job and a metric are named in the 90-day plan rather than claimed
  here.

## Out of scope

- Reconciliation (TD-0001), partitioning the entry table, expiring coins.
